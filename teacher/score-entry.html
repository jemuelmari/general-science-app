🏠 Dashboard 📊 Class Record 📋 Tracker 🖨️ Print Exam ✍️ Score Entry
For students who took the test on paper (no device / no internet), or to correct a score.
1) Print the test and answer sheet in Print Exam (same questions and order as online, Set A = ACADEMIC A, Set B = ACADEMIC B).   2) Check the papers.   3) Type the raw score here and click Save.
A saved score is marked PAPER and replaces the online score in the Class Record, Grading Sheet, Reports, Assessment Tracker and Intervention — it may also be lower. Remove it to go back to the online score. PT scores are out of 100 (the same as the rubric total).
