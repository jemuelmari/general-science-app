Term Access Control

Open or lock each term per section. Students cannot enter a locked term, even by direct link.

ℹ️
How it works
Toggling a term OPEN lets students enter it regardless of their progress. Toggling it LOCKED prevents new activity — students can still view their past progress in that term (read-only).
